  • Clicking a row opens a right-side drawer (a Sheet), so the list keeps its scroll, search and filters.

  • The Role column becomes an inline select for team admins and global admins. It calls the existing add-member endpoint with the new role, which already updates in place.
  • "Add members" lets you pick several people at once, with one role for all of them.
  • The row menu (RowActionsMenu) holds "Remove from team" and "View in People".
  • A one-line role legend under the table answers "what can an editor do?" where the question comes up.
