title: Collections & Saved Queries

Your **Library** holds saved queries; **collections** are shared playlists for organizing
them. Members run queries, editors change the shared query, and owners manage the
collection, but a collection never grants access to log data.

Open the Library from the sidebar. Collections sit in the left rail; selecting one shows its
queries, members, and shared teams on the right.

## Saved queries

A saved query is a named, reusable query bound to a single source. It stores the query text
(LogchefQL or SQL), and optionally the time range, limit, and variables.

Save one from the explorer with **Save**. The dialog asks for a name and lets you pick a
collection in the same step (defaulting to your personal collection), so saving and
organizing happen in one action.

Visibility follows **source access**: anyone who can reach the query's source (through any
team they belong to) can see and run it. Editing is more restricted; see
[Who can edit](#who-can-edit-a-query).

## Collections

A collection is a curated list of saved queries with its own members.

- **Personal collection**: every user has one, created automatically. It's private and can't
  be shared, renamed, or deleted. Think of it as your bookmarks.
- **Shared collections**: anyone can create one. You become its owner and can invite
  teammates or share it with whole teams. Use them for on-call runbooks, incident playbooks,
  or a team's go-to queries.

Create a collection with **New** in the Library rail, then **Invite** teammates or
**Share with team**, and pin queries to it.

## Roles

Shared collections have three roles:

| Role | Read &amp; run | Add/remove items | Edit the queries | Manage members and teams | Delete collection |
|------|:----------:|:----------------:|:----------------:|:------------------------:|:-----------------:|
| **Member** | ✓ | ✓ | — | — | — |
| **Editor** | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | — | — |
| **Owner**  | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |

Running, pinning, and editing always also require **source access**: a role in a collection
never grants access to the underlying logs. A query you can't run shows with a lock icon.
Removing an item from a collection needs no source access, so participants can unpin a
locked query.

## Share with a team

Owners can share a collection with a whole team instead of inviting people one by one.
Open the collection and choose **Share with team**.

- **Every current member of the team** sees the collection with the **Member** role. This
  includes team admins and editors: a team share never grants Editor or Owner rights. To
  give someone more, invite them directly; a direct role always takes precedence.
- **Membership is live.** People who join the team see the collection on their next request.
  People who leave the team lose it, unless they are direct members or belong to another
  shared team.
- **No source access is granted.** A team share controls who sees the collection, not who can
  read logs. Each person runs only the queries whose sources their own teams can reach;
  the other items show with a lock icon, and the server rejects any attempt to run them.
- **Which teams you can pick:** owners can share with teams they belong to. Global admins
  can share their own collections with any team. Being a global admin does not let you
  manage someone else's collection. Through an OAuth token the global admin role does not
  apply, so an admin can share only with their own teams and sees the lists only as an owner.
- **Removing a share** affects only that team. Direct members and other shared teams keep
  access. Owners can remove a share even after they leave the team. Deleting a team removes
  its shares automatically.
- Personal collections can't be shared with teams.

The member count shows direct members only (owners included); shared teams are listed and
counted separately. Only owners see the member and team lists. A global admin sees them only
when they also participate in the collection, directly or through a shared team, and still
needs to be an owner to change them. Other members and editors don't see either list. Leaving a collection removes your direct membership only; you keep
access through a shared team until you leave that team or the owner removes the share.

## Who can edit a query

A saved query can be edited by:

1. its **creator**,
2. a **global admin**, or
3. an **owner or editor** of a shared collection that contains it (still requiring source access).

Because a saved query is a single shared row, **editing it changes it everywhere it
appears**, in every collection and for every viewer. To make a variant instead of changing
the shared one, save a new query rather than editing.

Deleting a query is more restricted than editing: only the **creator** or a **global admin**
can delete it, since deletion removes it from every collection at once.

## Common workflows

**Share a team's go-to queries**

1. **New** → name the collection (e.g. "Payments on-call").
2. **Share with team** to give the whole team read & run access, or **Invite** individuals
   as Members (read & run) or Editors (can also refine the queries).
3. Open each query in the explorer and **Save** it into the collection.

**Hand a query to a teammate to maintain**

Add them to a shared collection containing the query as an **Editor**. They can now open it,
change it, and re-save without needing global-admin rights.
